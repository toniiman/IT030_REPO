About Us

House of Imooni was born in Lagos, Nigeria, in the early 2000s, blending heritage and contemporary fashion to create a unique take on luxury ready-to-wear. Rooted in the rich culture of its African origins, the brand is known for bold patterns and statement prints that captivate and inspire. With a global presence through select international stockists and specialty retailers, House of Imooni is dedicated to delivering stylish, high-quality pieces that are both versatile and timeless. As a small business, we take pride in crafting fashion that embodies elegance, comfort, and authenticity.
